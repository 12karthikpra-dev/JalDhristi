JalDrishti Explained in Simple Words

No technical jargon. Just clear concepts.

Imagine if a giant digital camera in space (a satellite) could watch every river, seasonal creek, and farm in your district. At the same time, a government officer or engineer on the ground takes a photo of a newly built check-dam or percolation pond on their smartphone.

👉 JalDrishti is the smart AI bridge between the satellite in the sky and the camera on the ground. It verifies that the water structure was genuinely built at the exact location, checks if it is choked by silt or functioning well, and proves via satellite whether real water is being stored and whether nearby agricultural land has turned greener over time.

How the System Works

Three complementary layers working together in harmony.

satellite_alt

1. The Sky (Satellites)

Sentinel-2 and ISRO Bhuvan satellites scan the watershed every 5 days. They capture invisible infrared light bands to measure NDVI (vegetation health) and NDWI (surface water spread) across millions of hectares.

smartphone

2. The Ground (Mobile App)

Field officers visit construction sites and snap photos. The mobile app automatically watermarks unforgeable GPS latitude/longitude, altitude, and timestamp metadata directly into the image file.

neurology

3. The AI Brain (The Engine)

Our computer vision models classify the structure, estimate siltation depth, and cross-match with satellite time-series data to calculate an objective Watershed Impact Score (e.g. 78/100).

The One-Line Working Principle
📸 Field Photo → 📍 GPS Location → 🛰️ Satellite Data → 🧠 AI Validation → 📊 Impact Score (0–100) → ✅ Fund Release

What We Are Aiming to Achieve

Four tangible outcomes for rural India's water security.

gavel

1. Stop 'Ghost' Projects & Ensure 100% Transparency

In traditional paper-based systems, funds can be disbursed for structures that were never actually built or built in the wrong spot. JalDrishti makes project verification cryptographically secure and unforgeable.

warning

2. Detect Siltation Before Dams Become Useless

When soil washes into check-dams, mud fills up the reservoir and the dam stops holding water. Our platform alerts district engineers months before the monsoon so desiltation drives can be carried out.

architecture

3. Science-Backed Dam Siting with AI

Instead of constructing dams based on guesswork or political convenience, our Check-Dam Siting Optimizer calculates terrain slopes and rainfall runoff patterns to place dams where they trap maximum water.

water

4. Recharging Groundwater for Marginal Farmers

The ultimate metric of success isn't concrete walls—it's whether farmers' borewells hold water during the dry months of April and May, allowing a second winter crop and preventing farmer distress.

Core Fundamentals & Ground Reality

Key scientific and operational concepts behind the platform.

terrain

The Watershed as a Natural Basin

A watershed is a natural geographical bowl. Whenever rain falls across ridges and hillsides, water travels through streams into a central river or underground aquifer. Managing this basin means trapping rainfall at the upper ridges before it rushes away and strips fertile soil.

water_lux

The Function of Small Check-Dams

Check-dams are small masonry or rock structures erected across seasonal torrents. Instead of halting a river forever, they decelerate swift monsoon runoff, allowing sediment to settle and giving water ample time to percolate deep underground to replenish irrigation wells.

verified_user

Dual-Layer Tamper Prevention

Field photos cannot be faked or reused from the internet. The system extracts hardware-level coordinates, azimuth angle, and timestamp from the device camera, then cross-verifies against orbital satellite radar passes to confirm physical water storage in real life.

speed

The Composite Watershed Impact Score

Rather than tracking bureaucratic paperwork, success is evaluated by an algorithmic 0–100 index. This combines satellite vegetation gains (+18%), surface water retention (+24%), soil erosion arrest (-12%), and verified masonry stability into one transparent score.
