The Watershed Challenge

Why Traditional Monitoring Falls Short

Manual reporting workflows introduce verification bottlenecks and hinder timely resource allocation across rural districts.

edit_off

Manual & Delayed Reporting

Paper logs and fragmented spreadsheets delay progress updates by weeks, making it impossible to respond dynamically to monsoon anomalies.

Lack of Verifiable Field Evidence

Without cryptographically secured geo-tagged photos and sensor feeds, verifying the physical construction of check-dams and contour trenches remains subjective.

How JalDrishti Works

A streamlined 5-step workflow bridging macro satellite intelligence with micro-level field verification.

STEP 01

Ingest Data

Automated ingestion of ISRO, Sentinel, and meteorological datasets.

STEP 02

Map Watershed

Delineation of micro-watersheds and drainage networks using DEM grids.

STEP 03

Capture Evidence

Field officers upload geo-tagged photographic proof of structural works.

STEP 04

AI Change Detection

Neural networks compare baseline vs current imagery for impact verification.

Comprehensive automated scorecards generated for government auditing.
